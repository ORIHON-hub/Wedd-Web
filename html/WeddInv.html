Getting there

  1. 1–1.5 hrs

    FLY TO CEBU

    Fly to Mactan-Cebu International Airport, then take a taxi or Grab to Cebu North Bus Terminal.

  2. 3–4 hrs

    BUS TO HAGNAYA

    Ride a Ceres Liner bus to Hagnaya Port, or arrange a private van.

  3. 1.5–2 hrs

    FERRY TO SANTA FE

    Take a ferry bound for Santa Fe, Bantayan Island.

  4. 10–20 min

    TRICYCLE

    Take a local tricycle or resort transfer to Santa Fe Beach Club.
